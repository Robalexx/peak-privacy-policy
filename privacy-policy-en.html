🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 17, 2025

At Peak, we take your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a Peak account, we collect:

  • Username and display name
  • Email address
  • Password (securely encrypted and stored)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Biography (optional)

1.2 User-Generated Content

  • Goals and exploits: Titles, descriptions, categories, durations
  • Posts: Photos and videos you share to document your progress
  • Comments and interactions: Likes, comments, messages
  • Sub-tasks: Steps of your goals

1.3 Usage Data

  • Navigation data: Pages visited, features used
  • Progress statistics: Completion percentage, time spent
  • Social interactions: Follows, collaborations
  • Technical information: Device type, operating system, app version

1.4 Location Data

Peak does NOT collect geolocation data.

2. How We Use Your Data

2.1 Service Provision

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable goal tracking
  • Display your profile and public exploits
  • Facilitate social interactions (follows, collaborations)

2.2 Service Improvement

  • Analyze app usage to improve user experience
  • Identify and fix bugs
  • Develop new features

2.3 Communication

  • Send you notifications related to your goals
  • Inform you of social interactions (likes, comments)
  • Communicate important app updates

3. Data Sharing

3.1 With Other Users

  • Public profile: Username, photo, bio, public exploits
  • Private exploits: Visible only to you and invited collaborators
  • Posts: Visible according to your privacy settings

3.2 With Third Parties

We NEVER sell your personal data to third parties.

We only share data with:

  • Supabase: Our backend infrastructure for secure data storage
  • Cloud service providers: For media storage (photos, videos)
  • Analytics services: Anonymized data to improve the app (Amplitude)

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption: All communications are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Passwords: Hashed with secure algorithms
  • Authentication: Secure system with access tokens
  • Access control: Only authorized personnel can access data

4.2 Retention Period

  • Active account: As long as your account is active
  • Deleted account: 30 days after deletion (to allow recovery)
  • Anonymized data: Retained indefinitely for global statistics

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Access all your data via your profile
  • Rectification: Modify your information in settings
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all your data
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in JSON format
  • Opposition: Object to processing for marketing purposes

To Exercise These Rights

Contact us at: privacy@peak-app.com

6. Children's Data

Peak is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email.

8. Contact

Email: privacy@peak-app.com
Support: support@peak-app.com
